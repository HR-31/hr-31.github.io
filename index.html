Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal Mac assistant with a companion phone app for notes, meetings, tasks and daily planning. This build is maintained by Harish Ravi for personal use and a small number of invited users.

Connect Google Calendar

Jarvis connects directly to Google Calendar after you sign in with Google and approve access. It reads your primary calendar to show upcoming events and support meeting preparation and daily planning. This connection does not create, change or delete Google Calendar events.

Google account identity is used to show which account is connected. Google access and refresh tokens are stored in your Mac’s Keychain. The phone receives calendar information through its paired Mac.

Calendar connection is independent of an AI subscription. Some AI features may send relevant calendar information to the configured AI provider; see the privacy notice for details.

Connect Gmail

The optional native Gmail connector reads one selected account, shows threads and supports approved label filing, plain-text drafts and sends. Reading starts with read-only permission. Filing, draft saving and sending require a permission upgrade. Every manual send is approved through an exact preview; optional editable automation rules can authorise automatic replies and can be paused.

Email AI processing is optional and sends selected email evidence to Claude for briefs and suggestions. Native retrieval uses Google directly, with credentials stored in your Mac’s Keychain. The phone continues to receive bounded summaries only. Outlook work-account support is separate future work.
