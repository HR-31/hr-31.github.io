Jarvis Privacy Policy

This notice describes the native Google Calendar connection in Harish Ravi’s personal build of Jarvis.

Information accessed

With your permission, Jarvis receives your Google account email address and reads calendar events. Event information can include titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, organisers and attendees. The current integration reads your primary calendar. It requests read-only Calendar permission and account identity; it does not request Gmail access.

How information is used

Calendar information supports upcoming-event displays, meeting reminders, meeting preparation and daily planning. Your account email identifies the connected account in settings. Google access and refresh tokens let Jarvis maintain the connection without repeatedly asking you to sign in.

Storage and sharing

Account tokens are stored in your Mac’s Keychain. Calendar information and information derived from it may be held in Jarvis’s local data, saved briefs, notes and backups, and synchronised to your paired phone.

Some Jarvis AI features use remote AI services, including Anthropic’s Claude through the account configured on your Mac. AI-assisted meeting features may send calendar-derived participant names and relevant meeting content to your configured AI provider. Calendar displays, daily calendar summaries and pre-meeting history lookups themselves run locally. A native Calendar connection does not make those AI features local-only. OAuth credentials are not included in AI prompts.

This connection does not route your calendar through a developer-operated account-collection service. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Jarvis itself does not train general-purpose AI models. Your configured AI provider handles submitted information under its own terms and your account settings; this connection does not change those settings. Jarvis’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect Google Calendar in Jarvis settings or revoke Jarvis’s access in your Google Account. Disconnecting removes the local connection credentials and attempts to revoke Google access. It does not automatically erase previously saved notes, briefs, backups or copies already synchronised to your phone. Remove saved information using the relevant Jarvis controls and manage your device backups separately.

Your choices and contact

Connecting is optional. You choose the Google account and approve the requested permissions on Google’s own page. Contact mr.miyagi.coaching@gmail.com with questions about this build or its handling of Google data.
