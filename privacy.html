Jarvis Privacy Policy

This notice describes the native Google Calendar and Gmail connections in Harish Ravi’s personal build of Jarvis.

Information accessed

With your permission, Jarvis receives your Google account email address and reads calendar events. Event information can include titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, organisers and attendees. The current integration reads your primary calendar. Calendar requests read-only permission and account identity. Gmail initially requests reading permission and authenticated account identity; enabling filing, Gmail draft saving or sending upgrades to Gmail modify permission. Gmail information can include message headers, labels, snippets and selected message text. Attachments are not downloaded automatically.

How information is used

Calendar information supports upcoming-event displays, meeting reminders, meeting preparation and daily planning. Gmail supports reading conversations, approved label filing, plain-text drafts and sending. Manual sends require approval of the exact preview. Optional editable rules can authorise automatic drafting or sending for matching new mail; rules start disabled and can be edited or paused. Your authenticated account identity identifies the connected account in settings. Google access and refresh tokens let Jarvis maintain the connection without repeatedly asking you to sign in.

Storage and sharing

Account tokens are stored in your Mac’s Keychain. Calendar information and information derived from it may be held in Jarvis’s local data, saved briefs, notes and backups, and synchronised to your paired phone. Gmail messages, draft text, rules and an operation record are stored locally with account ownership. The paired phone receives only the existing bounded email summary; full messages, approvals and sending are not available on the phone.

Some Jarvis AI features use remote AI services, including Anthropic’s Claude through the account configured on your Mac. AI-assisted meeting features may send calendar-derived participant names and relevant meeting content to your configured AI provider. Calendar displays, daily calendar summaries and pre-meeting history lookups themselves run locally. A native Calendar connection does not make those AI features local-only. Optional email AI processing sends selected message text and headers to your configured Claude service for briefs, filing suggestions and reply preparation. Native Gmail reading and manual composing work without AI. Separate controls govern native polling and AI processing; enabling a reply rule starts its required polling while Jarvis is running. Email text goes to AI as evidence and cannot authorise actions or change recipients or rules. OAuth credentials are not included in AI prompts.

This connection does not route your calendar through a developer-operated account-collection service. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Jarvis itself does not train general-purpose AI models. Your configured AI provider handles submitted information under its own terms and your account settings; this connection does not change those settings. Jarvis’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect either connector locally in Jarvis settings or revoke Jarvis’s access in your Google Account. Local disconnect removes that connector’s credentials and active projection, and retires work that has not been dispatched. A separately labelled Remove Google access action revokes access for the Google subject/project and can affect both Calendar and Gmail. A send already dispatched may complete; uncertain sends remain in the operation record for reconciliation rather than automatic retry. It does not automatically erase previously saved notes, briefs, backups or copies already synchronised to your phone. Clear downloaded mail removes eligible local downloaded mail separately from disconnect. Pending drafts and unresolved operations are retained until resolved or explicitly discarded. Saved project evidence follows its own retention and keeps its source-account identity. An offline phone may retain its old summary until it reconnects and receives the updated projection. Remove saved information using the relevant Jarvis controls and manage your device backups separately.

Your choices and contact

Connecting is optional. You choose the Google account and approve the requested permissions on Google’s own page. Contact mr.miyagi.coaching@gmail.com with questions about this build or its handling of Google data.
